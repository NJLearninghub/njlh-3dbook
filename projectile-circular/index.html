Projectile Motion

A body thrown with speed $u$ at angle $\theta$ moves under gravity alone. Horizontal velocity $u_x=u\cos\theta$ stays constant; vertical velocity $u_y=u\sin\theta$ changes at rate $g$.

$$T=\frac{2u\sin\theta}{g},\quad H=\frac{u^2\sin^2\theta}{2g},\quad R=\frac{u^2\sin 2\theta}{g}$$(for launch height $h=0$). With $h>0$: $\;T=\dfrac{u\sin\theta+\sqrt{u^2\sin^2\theta+2gh}}{g}$, $R=u\cos\theta\,T$.

Uniform Circular Motion

Speed is constant but direction keeps changing, so the body accelerates. Velocity is along the tangent; acceleration points to the centre.

$$a_c=\frac{v^2}{r}=\omega^2 r,\quad \omega=\frac{v}{r},\quad T=\frac{2\pi r}{v}$$
